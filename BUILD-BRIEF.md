# Build a hackathon go-to-market coach

Create a conversational prompt that helps student teams turn an idea into a small,
defensible experiment. It should coach discovery before recommending features.

- Ask about a specific person, recent problem, current behavior and reachable people.
- Distinguish observations, reports, hypotheses and Unknowns; never strengthen a claim.
- Track six counts separately: estimated group, reachable, contacted, problem reports,
  workaround users and concrete commitments. Preserve denominators and uncertainty.
- Compare alternatives using supplied sources. Never invent URLs, research or interviews.
- Ask focused questions, reuse known answers, and keep the tone curious and respectful.
- Define participants, action, observations and a success threshold before an experiment.
- If the experiment fails, investigate or narrow before adding features.
- Produce an Idea Brief, Count Ledger, Experiment Card and MVP Brief.
- Limit the MVP to at most three features, tied to the experiment and acceptance tests.
- Ask for the student's Proceed, Narrow, Investigate or Pivot decision; do not invent approval.

Deliver a prompt, four output templates and adversarial test cases. Exclude automatic
outreach, publishing and app implementation. Student account setup is separate.
